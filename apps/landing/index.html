01Relay

The relay is a pipe.
It cannot read what it carries.

Your phone and your machine agree on keys with a Noise IK handshake, then seal every record with ChaCha20-Poly1305 and rekey every 220 messages. The relay, on Cloudflare Workers, matches the two ends by host id and copies bytes between them. It keeps no accounts and stores no messages.

relay-doriface.omo.devciphertext

no key, no plaintext, no storage

What the relay can see

  • which host ids open sessions to which, and when
  • the addresses they connect from
  • the size and timing of encrypted records

What it never sees

  • your keystrokes and terminal output
  • pairing secrets, device names, mesh records
  • anything it could forge, replay or splice

02Pairing

Pair a phone in one scan.
The secret never leaves the link.

The pairing secret lives in the link's #fragment, which a browser never sends to any server. No camera handy? Type the 8-character code instead. CPace keeps the relay from testing guesses offline, and the host answers at most 10 tries per offer.

  1. Run one command on the machineIt prints a QR code, a link, a code and the host's fingerprint.
  2. Scan it with your phoneThe camera opens the pair page with everything filled in.
  3. Compare the fingerprintTen groups of four, from the host's Noise key. A changed key is refused unless you say so.

03Mesh

Machines that vouch for each other.
No ssh, no open port.

Put your laptop, a build box and a VPS in one mesh and each one shows the others in its sidebar. Membership is a set of records every member signs with its Ed25519 key and syncs over the same encrypted relay. The relay stores none of it and can forge none of it.

  1. 01Four machines share one mesh. Each one's hc lists the other three as machines.
  2. 02mesh pair on laptop invites a fifth machine; pi asks to join.
  3. 03You approve on laptop. The signed admit record syncs, and every member dials pi.
  4. 04mesh revoke vps --compromised: every member closes its sessions and refuses its keys.

Approved, then admitted

A machine joins only after you approve it on a member. For seven days every member shows who joined, who invited it and its fingerprint.

One sidebar, every machine

Each member adds every other member as a herdr machine on its own, so their spaces and agents appear under their names.

Revoke in one record

With --compromised, one record removes a member and every machine it admitted. Its sessions close and its keys are refused on every path.

04Platforms

Desktop, browser, phone.
The same terminal on each.

Terminals render with libghostty, built from Ghostty's own source and compiled to WebAssembly, so a pane on your phone draws the same cells as on your desktop.

Desktop app

Starts the host and herdr-core on this computer, installs herdr-core for you when it is missing, and offers this computer to your phone.

macOS, Windows, Linux

Any modern browser

Open a tab, pair, and every pane of every machine is there. Nothing to install on a borrowed computer.

Chrome and Edge 119+, Safari 17.4+, Firefox 121+

Your phone

Install it from the browser as an app. A key bar puts ctrl, esc and the arrows under your thumb, so you can answer an agent from anywhere.

iOS 17.4+, Android with Chrome 119+
on your machine 
$ doriface-host pair                     # a QR code, a link and a code for your phone
$ doriface-host mesh pair                # invite another machine into your mesh
$ doriface-host mesh join <code>         # on that machine: ask to join
$ doriface-host mesh revoke <member>     # remove a member everywhere
